Why Scott

Good coaching starts
with your game.

An individual approach to technique, strategy, and the way you learn.

The approach

More than a better stroke.

Scott brings more than three decades of teaching experience and continues to compete nationally at the highest level. Staying active as both a player and coach keeps his teaching connected to the way the game is played today.

He brings that perspective to your game, starting with what feels reliable, what breaks down, and what you want to improve. Lessons combine modern techniques and strategies with focused practice and feedback you can use in match play.

Bay Area roots

A family rooted in tennis.

Born and raised in San Francisco, Scott graduated from San Francisco State University with a bachelor’s degree in Broadcast Communications.

Scott comes from a well-known and respected Bay Area tennis family. His father, Gil, has won 13 national championships and was #1 in the world in the Men’s 55 & over division. His brothers, Mike and Kevin, were both ranked in the ATP world top 500, and his sister, Susan, played #1 at San Francisco State University.
